# AquaBot – Mathematical Modeling & Hydrodynamics Specification

This document details the governing equations, numerical integration schemes, thruster allocation matrices, and potential field mathematics implemented in **AquaBot**.

---

## 1. Marine Rigid-Body Hydrodynamics (Fossen Formulation)

The 6-DOF dynamic equations of motion for an underwater vehicle are expressed as:

$$\mathbf{M} \dot{\boldsymbol{\nu}} + \mathbf{C}(\boldsymbol{\nu})\boldsymbol{\nu} + \mathbf{D}(\boldsymbol{\nu})\boldsymbol{\nu} + \mathbf{g}(\boldsymbol{\eta}) = \boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{thruster}} + \boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{external}}$$

Where:
- $\boldsymbol{\eta} = [x, y, z, \phi, \theta, \psi]^T$: Position and orientation in the earth-fixed NED frame.
- $\boldsymbol{\nu} = [u, v, w, p, q, r]^T$: Linear and angular velocities in the body-fixed frame (Surge, Sway, Heave, Roll rate, Pitch rate, Yaw rate).
- $\mathbf{M} = \mathbf{M}_{RB} + \mathbf{M}_{A}$: Total inertia matrix comprising rigid-body mass $\mathbf{M}_{RB}$ and hydrodynamic added mass $\mathbf{M}_{A}$ representing water accelerated with the hull.
- $\mathbf{D}(\boldsymbol{\nu})$: Non-linear hydrodynamic damping matrix.
- $\mathbf{g}(\boldsymbol{\eta})$: Hydrostatic restoring forces (gravity vs buoyancy).
- $\boldsymbol{\tau}_{\text{thruster}}$: Net forces and moments generated by thrusters.

---

## 2. Decoupled 4-DOF Dynamics & Damping

For an actively stabilized underwater ROV with symmetrical geometry, motion is decoupled along the active degrees of freedom:

### Surge Motion ($u$):
$$(m + m_{a,x})\dot{u} = T_x - D_{l,x} u - D_{q,x} |u|u$$

### Sway Motion ($v$):
$$(m + m_{a,y})\dot{v} = T_y - D_{l,y} v - D_{q,y} |v|v$$

### Heave Motion ($w$):
$$(m + m_{a,z})\dot{w} = T_z - D_{l,z} w - D_{q,z} |w|w + (B - W)$$
Where $(B - W) = +0.8\text{ N}$ models slight positive buoyancy failsafe.

### Yaw Rotation ($r$):
$$(I_z + I_{a,z})\dot{r} = \tau_z - D_{l,\psi} r - D_{q,\psi} |r|r$$

### Roll ($\phi$) and Pitch ($\theta$) Restoring Dynamics:
Due to low center of gravity (ballast lead weights at keel and syntactic foam at top), hydrostatic restoring torque provides strong passive pendulum self-righting:
$$\ddot{\phi} = -k_{\text{roll}}\phi - d_{\text{roll}}\dot{\phi}$$
$$\ddot{\theta} = -k_{\text{pitch}}\theta - d_{\text{pitch}}\dot{\theta}$$

---

## 3. Kinematic Transformation

Body velocities $[u, v, w]^T$ are transformed to earth-fixed frame rates $[\dot{x}, \dot{y}, \dot{z}]^T$ via the rotation matrix $\mathbf{R}_b^n(\psi)$:

$$\begin{bmatrix} \dot{x} \\ \dot{y} \\ \dot{z} \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\psi & -\sin\psi & 0 \\ \sin\psi & \cos\psi & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} u \\ v \\ w \end{bmatrix}$$

$$\dot{\psi} = r$$

---

## 4. Vectored 6-Thruster Allocation Matrix

AquaBot features four horizontal vectored thrusters mounted at $45^\circ$ angles ($\alpha = \pi/4$) and two vertical heave thrusters:

$$\begin{bmatrix} T_x \\ T_y \\ T_z \\ \tau_z \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix}
\cos 45^\circ & \cos 45^\circ & \cos 45^\circ & \cos 45^\circ & 0 & 0 \\
-\sin 45^\circ & \sin 45^\circ & \sin 45^\circ & -\sin 45^\circ & 0 & 0 \\
0 & 0 & 0 & 0 & 1 & 1 \\
-L & L & -L & L & 0 & 0
\end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} T_1 \\ T_2 \\ T_3 \\ T_4 \\ T_5 \\ T_6 \end{bmatrix}$$

Where:
- $L = (d_x + d_y) \cdot \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$ is the effective torque moment arm.
- Individual thrusters undergo first-order electromechanical motor lag:
  $$\dot{T}_i = \frac{T_{i,\text{target}} - T_i}{\tau_{\text{motor}}}, \quad \tau_{\text{motor}} = 0.12\text{ s}$$

## 5. Artificial Potential Field (APF) Obstacle Avoidance

Autonomous pathing synthesizes virtual attractive and repulsive forces:

### Attractive Potential (Goal Waypoint):
$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{att}} = k_{\text{att}} \cdot \frac{\mathbf{p}_{\text{target}} - \mathbf{p}}{\|\mathbf{p}_{\text{target}} - \mathbf{p}\|}$$

### Repulsive Potential (Sonar Acoustic Beams):
For every sonar beam $i$ detecting an obstacle within the influence threshold $D_{\text{inf}} = 5.5\text{ m}$:

$$\mathbf{F}_{\text{rep}, i} = k_{\text{rep}} \left( \frac{1}{d_i} - \frac{1}{D_{\text{inf}}} \right) \frac{1}{d_i^2} \cdot (-\hat{\mathbf{u}}_{\text{beam}, i})$$

When the obstacle is dead ahead ($|\theta_{\text{beam}}| < 10^\circ$), a lateral maritime starboard-turn bias is added to break collinear symmetry and prevent local minima deadlocks.

If minimum clearance drops below $D_{\text{critical}} = 1.6\text{ m}$, emergency braking and negative surge thrust ($u_{\text{cmd}} = -0.4$) are triggered.

---

## 6. Closed-Loop PID Control

The discrete PID law with anti-windup clamping and derivative filtering:

$$u(k) = K_p e(k) + K_i \sum_{j=0}^k e(j)\Delta t + K_d \frac{e(k) - e(k-1)}{\Delta t}$$

For heading tracking, angle wrap-around normalization is enforced:
$$e_\psi(k) = \text{atan2}(\sin(\psi_{\text{des}} - \psi), \cos(\psi_{\text{des}} - \psi)) \in [-\pi, \pi]$$
